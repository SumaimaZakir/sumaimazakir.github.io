Process

How We Build Together

A structured 6-step methodology designed to take your project from initial concept to digital launch.

01

Discovery

We discuss your goals.

We begin with a consultation to understand business goals, target audience, positioning, and deliverables.

02

Research

Understanding the market.

Competitor benchmarks, user needs, and market research help identify useful opportunities for differentiation.

03

Design

Crafting visual concepts.

Wireframes, mood boards, and polished UI screens are developed in Figma with feedback built into the process.

04

Development

Building clean, responsive code.

Approved designs are translated into responsive websites with clean structure, CMS controls where needed, and thoughtful interactions.

05

Testing

Checking quality & usability.

Responsive checks, browser testing, performance review, SEO basics, and final quality checks are completed before release.

06

Launch

Deploying & moving forward.

Final deployment, analytics setup, handover, and optional ongoing support help keep the project moving after launch.
